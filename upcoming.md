# DevTrack: Upcoming Features & Roadmap 🚀

This document outlines the vision for DevTrack's future evolution. We are committed to building the ultimate ecosystem for developers to track, showcase, and accelerate their careers.

## 1. AI-Powered Hackathon PPT Maker 📊
*Transform your GitHub projects into professional pitch decks in seconds.*
- **Context Awareness**: Automatically pulls project descriptions, tech stacks, and AI analysis from your DevTrack projects.
- **Stunning Templates**: Modern, developer-centric slide designs tailored for hackathon judges.
- **Feature Highlights**: Auto-generates slides for "The Problem," "Our Solution," "Tech Stack," and "Future Roadmap."
- **Export Options**: Export to PDF, Google Slides, or an interactive web-based presentation.

## 2. Advanced Resume Customization 📄
*More than just a template—a dynamic career storytelling tool.*
- **Granular Layout Control**: Drag-and-drop sections to prioritize what matters most (Experience vs. Projects vs. Skills).
- **Multiple Profiles**: Create specific resumes for different roles (e.g., Frontend React, Backend Node, Fullstack).
- **Direct GitHub Integration**: Real-time stats from your GitHub (contributions, top languages) embedded directly.
- **ATS Optimization**: AI suggestions to make your resume more compatible with Applicant Tracking Systems.

## 3. Intelligent Team Matchmaking 🤝 
*Stop searching for teammates; let AI find them for you.*
- **Complementary Skills**: Find collaborators who have the skills you're missing (e.g., find a Backend expert if you're a Frontend pro).
- **Project-Specific Search**: Post a project idea and let AI suggest local or global DevTrack users who would be a perfect fit.
- **Compatibility Score**: Based on shared tech interests and coding frequency.

## 4. Developer Portfolio Generator 🌐 
*Turn your DevTrack profile into a high-performance portfolio website.*
- **One-Click Deploy**: Instantly generate a hosted `devtrack.to/username` site.
- **Dynamic Projects**: Your showcase projects are automatically synced and displayed with live metrics.
- **Premium Themes**: Choose from glassmorphism, terminal-style, or minimal professional designs.

## 5. Mock Interview AI 🤖 
*Practice interviews based on the actual code you've written.*
- **Code-Based Questions**: AI analyzes your projects and asks questions about your architecture and choices.
- **Tech Stack Specific**: Practice React hooks, SQL optimization, or system design based on your project history.
- **Performance Feedback**: Get detailed feedback on your explanations and technical depth.

---
*DevTrack is built for developers, by developers. Stay tuned for more updates!*
